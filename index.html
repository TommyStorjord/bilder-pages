Bilder

A photographic slideshow for a Samsung television, and a phone app to start it.

What it is

Bilder is a private, non-commercial application built for one household. It shows a family photograph collection on a television in the living room, with the caption written under each picture, and it is operated from a phone by somebody in their nineties. It is not distributed, not sold, and not available in any app store.

What it does

  • Shows photographs from a Google Photos album on a Samsung TV, one at a time, with their descriptions.
  • Plays video files from a USB drive connected to the television.
  • Wakes the television over the local network and starts the slideshow, from a phone on the same network.

The Google account data it uses

Bilder requests a single scope, photoslibrary.readonly.appcreateddata. That scope grants read access only to media the application itself created — it cannot see the rest of the library. In practice this is the album uploaded by the household's own companion tool, and nothing else.

It reads album names, photograph descriptions, and the temporary URLs needed to display the images. It never writes, deletes, shares or uploads anything.

Where the data goes

To the television on the home network, and nowhere else. There is no server, no account, no analytics and no third party. See the privacy policy for the detail.
